# Code of conduct

cerne follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). It applies to every space of the project: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other place where you represent it.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, critique the work and not the person, and accept that the maintainer has the final say on what fits the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, reach the maintainer, [Henrique Sakata](https://github.com/henriquesakata), privately rather than in public. Reports are handled in confidence, and the enforcement guidelines of the Covenant apply: from a private warning up to a permanent ban, depending on what happened.
